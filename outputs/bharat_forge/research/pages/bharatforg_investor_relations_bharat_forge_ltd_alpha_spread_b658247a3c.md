**Results:** Bharat Forge ended FY26 with consolidated revenue of INR 16,812 crores and EBITDA of INR 2,921 crores, up 11% and 6% respectively, while net debt-to-equity stayed low at 0.41.


**Growth drivers:** Management said the strongest growth in FY27 should come from Aerospace, then Defence, then the Components business, with India operations expected to grow close to 25% plus if geopolitical conditions do not worsen.


**Defence ramp-up:** The company highlighted an order book of close to INR 11,000 crores and said major milestones this year will be ATAGS FAT completion, ATAGS production ramp-up, and CQB carbine production.


**Overseas restructuring:** Bharat Forge has started restructuring its German steel forging business, CDP Bharat Forge, and expects the process to take 15 to 18 months, which should reduce losses from overseas operations over time.


**Capital allocation:** Management said it will invest INR 800 crores to INR 850 crores of capex over 15 to 18 months, while also writing off some e-mobility investments where it does not see near-term business ramp-up.


**New bets:** Aerospace is becoming a much bigger business, now about 26% of industrial exports in Q4, while recent moves including K-Drive, Fortuna Engineering, and external capital for JSA are aimed at speeding growth in selected niches.
