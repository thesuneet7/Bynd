### Company overview

_Source: [screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/BHARATFORG/consolidated/)_

**About**

Bharat Forge is engaged in the manufacturing and selling of forged and machined Compoundant for auto and industry sector.(Source : 201903 Annual Report Page No: 123)

**Key points**

- **Part of Kalyani Group:** The company belongs to the Kalyani Group which has an annual turnover of more than USD 3 Billion. Apart from the forging Industry, the group has a presence in engineering steel, automotive, industrial, renewable energy, urban infrastructure, and specialty chemicals and maintains a leading position in various business segments.
- **Market Position:** The company is India’s largest manufacturer and exporter of auto components and is amongst the world’s leading powertrain and chassis components manufacturers.
- **1) Forgings (85% in FY24 vs 96% in FY22):** The company produces steel forgings, finished machined crankshafts, front axles assembly and components, road wheel, transmission parts, seal rings, clamp, hub, lightweight components, etc for automotive and industrial sectors. This segment grew by 35% between FY22 and FY24.
- **2) Defense & Others (15% in FY24 vs 4% in FY22%):** The defense arm, Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited (KSSL), offers artillery systems, protected vehicles, armored vehicles upgrades, ammunition, air defense solutions, and defense electronics publications. The defense segment recorded a growth of 280% between FY22 and FY24, driven by a robust order book.
- **Geographical Split:** Europe: 35% in FY24 vs 47% in FY22 USA: 25% in FY24 vs 19% in FY22 India: 24% in FY24 vs 25% in FY22 RoW: 16% in FY24 vs 9% in FY22
- **Order Book & Inflows:** In FY23 and FY24, the company recorded an order inflow of Rs. 10,200 Cr, including Rs. 6,494 Cr from defense and the remaining Rs. 3,706 Cr from the core business. As of FY24, it has an executable order book of Rs. 5,192 Cr in the defense segment.
- **Partnership:** In October 2024, KSSL partnered with US-based AM General & Mandus Group to co-develop and co-produce next-generation artillery platforms with advanced recoil technology for global markets.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** The company has 15 manufacturing locations spread across India, Germany, Sweden, France, and North America with a capacity of: Steel Forging: 6,43,750 MTPA Aluminum Casting: 52,400 MTPA Iron Casting: 77,760 MTPA
- **Expansion:** The company is expanding its defense capacity to manufacture over 250 guns and 1,000 vehicles per annum, the new plant will commence operations in Q3 FY25.  It will invest Rs. 1,000 Cr on capex in FY25-FY26.
- **Key point:** In FY24, the company established an AI/Digital manufacturing center to enhance its engineering and manufacturing processes. This initiative aims to improve productivity and shorten the time-to-market for its products.
- **JS Auto Cast:** The company entered into the industrial ferrous castings space by acquiring JS Autocast Foundry India Pvt Ltd for Rs 489.63 Cr in July 2022. In FY24, the liquid metal capacity at J S Auto Cast increased to 130,000 MTPA. It also completed the acquisition of the SEZ unit of Indo-Shell Mould at SIPCOT, Coimbatore, to scale up J S Auto Cast's automotive business.
- **Patents:** The company has a portfolio of 97 patent families with 71 jurisdiction-wise grants and 10 designs with 3 registered designs. In FY24, it was granted 41 patents and received 1 design registration.
- **Fund Raise:** In August 2024, the board approved a fund raise of Rs. 2,000 via allotment of equity instruments, to be solely used for growth-oriented initiatives in India.
- **Focus:** At a consolidated level, the company expects growth in FY25 to be driven by its defense business, industrial casting business, and improved capacity utilization in its overseas operations. Additionally, it anticipates a turnaround in its overseas business and margin improvement across other business verticals.
