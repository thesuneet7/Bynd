# Light Vehicles Business Unit

# Light Vehicles Business Unit

We are the leading supplier of hydraulic braking systems for passenger and small commercial vehicles. We cater to most Indian and multinational vehicle manufacturers. Our key products include callipers, drum brakes, actuation, slip control, brake hoses and clutch actuation.​
