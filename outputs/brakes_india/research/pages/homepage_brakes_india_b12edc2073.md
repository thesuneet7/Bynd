About TSF Group

Brakes India is promoted by the TSF Group, whose heritage dates back to 1911. The group’s interests span the automotive and financial services sectors.








Auto Components

Comprise Axles India, Brakes India, Wheels India, Turbo Energy Limited, Sundaram Hydraulics, Leggero Composites & Dunes Oman.








Auto Dealerships

Comprise Sundaram Motors, Madras Auto Service & Indian Motor Parts and Accessories (IMPAL).








Finance & Insurance

Comprise Sundaram Finance, Royal Sundaram General Insurance, Sundaram Home Finance, Sundaram Asset Management Company and Sundaram Finance Holdings.
