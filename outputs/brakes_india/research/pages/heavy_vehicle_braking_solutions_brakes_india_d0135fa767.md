# Heavy Vehicles Business Unit

# Heavy Vehicles Business Unit

We are the leading brake system supplier to major commercial vehicle, tractor and off-highway vehicle manufacturers in India. Alongside being the largest supplier of S Cam brakes in the world, our product range includes hydraulic brakes, clutch actuation, electro-magnetic retarders and air actuation (air management devices, control valves and wheel-end solutions).
